# LLM Wiki（LLM 維護的知識 Wiki）

Learning cluster: [[AI Agent Learning/04 - Advanced Knowledge/04 - Advanced Knowledge Overview|Advanced Knowledge overview]]

## Overview

Here, a readable, persistent wiki whose concept pages and links are drafted or maintained with an LLM. The term does not denote one universal standard.

## Key Concepts

**Why it matters:** It turns repeated reading into accumulated, inspectable knowledge.

- **citations**（引用）
- **revisions**（修訂）

## How It Works

1. Keep raw sources
2. Draft concept pages with **citations**
3. Review claims and links
4. Record **revisions**
5. Update pages when sources change.

## Example

After reading three retrieval papers, maintain pages for RAG, embeddings, and reranking, each linked to source material and related concepts.

## Common Pitfalls

> [!warning] Watch for this
> - Summaries can erase nuance or propagate mistakes.
> - This folder is an initial wiki; ongoing ingestion and automatic maintenance require a separate process.
